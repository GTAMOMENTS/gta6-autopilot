# Background music (optional)

Drop royalty-free music files here (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`, `.ogg`) and every video gets one of them,
chosen at random, as a quiet beat under the voice (volume: `config.json → video.fx.music_volume`).

Only use music you're allowed to use on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and X - for example tracks from the
YouTube Audio Library marked "no attribution required". Copyrighted songs will get videos muted or blocked.

With no files here, videos simply have no music (game audio + voice + sound effects).
